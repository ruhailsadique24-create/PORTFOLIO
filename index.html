Context

Beyond the CV

I am an aspiring cybersecurity professional who enjoys understanding how attacks happen, how defenders detect them, and how investigations are actually carried out.

My learning approach is strongly hands-on. Rather than stopping at theory, I use security labs, investigations, competitions and personal projects to turn concepts into practical skills.

My current interests include security monitoring, threat detection, incident investigation, digital forensics and security automation. I am particularly interested in the day-to-day problem solving involved in security operations.

The projects on this site are intended to show that practical process: what I investigated, why I approached a problem a certain way, what I learned and what evidence I produced.
